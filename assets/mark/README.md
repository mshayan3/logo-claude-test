# The mark

`marauders.svg` is the live direction: the roster skull, redrawn.

## What carried over

The silhouette family, the menace, the weight, one ink, full contrast, and the
stacked composition — wordmark above, mark beneath. This is an evolution of the
logo, not a replacement for it.

## What changed, and why

| Change | Reason |
|--------|--------|
| Teeth pulled up inside the jaw — five, even, nothing hanging below the silhouette | The long uneven teeth dropping past the jaw are *the* Punisher identifier. As long as they are the signature, the mark is a derivative. |
| Cranium redrawn taller than wide, with a proper temple and cheek taper | The old dome was Marvel's proportion — very wide, very flat. |
| Eye sockets rebuilt as five-sided angular cuts | Keeps the heavy brow and the downward inner angle. Drops the long swept wedge with the curved outer sweep. |
| A nasal aperture added | The noseless face is part of what makes the Punisher read instantly. |
| Cheek wings removed; zygomatic facets cut into the silhouette instead | Same structural job, different form. |

Drawn with real curves on a 200-unit grid: the cranium is two cubic beziers,
the cheeks and jaw are hard facets. It is built to hold up at 16px and to cut
cleanly for embroidery and vinyl.

## Type

Wordmark: **Saira Condensed 800** — squarish, condensed, sporty, close in
character to the original lettering but better built. Technical layer:
**JetBrains Mono**.

The slice through the wordmark is the old mark's stencil cut, regularised to a
single disciplined band instead of several arbitrary ones. Final letterforms
still need drawing as custom outlines; what is shown is the type with a slice
standing in.
