# Marauder — identity system (working draft)

Status: **concept stage.** Three directions on the table, one recommended.
Nothing here is final until the name clears a trademark search.

---

## Positioning

Marauder is an **advisory firm**, not a roster. The audience is org owners,
investors, sponsors, players and agents — B2B wearing B2C clothes.

Esports identity has collapsed into one language: angular shields, chrome
gradients, snarling animals, neon on black. A consultancy dressed as a team
competes with its own clients and forfeits the one thing it sells: outside
perspective.

**The strategy is restraint as differentiation.** Look like a firm. Keep enough
craft and game-native detail that nobody mistakes you for outsiders in suits.

## The name

Legacy roster name: *The Marauders.*

Recommended brand name: **MARAUDER**, singular, no definite article, with a
locked descriptor line.

    MARAUDER
    ESPORTS ADVISORY

"The Marauders" names people on a server. "Marauder" names an entity. The
scene's recognition of the name survives intact; the business register changes.
The roster name stays alive as heritage — *founded by the Marauders roster* —
in bios and deck openers, not on the door.

**Open item:** trademark search. "Marauders" is a crowded term across sports,
fiction and esports. Clear it before anything is printed.

**Recommended: 01, with the M set inside the frame** — `assets/primary/mark.svg`

The frame is the only one of the three that carries a system rather than just a
picture. The centre is a slot: it holds the M by default, and a client org's
mark in partnership lockups. The consultancy literally frames the client. That
is the business model drawn.

### Known weakness, stated plainly

Concept 03 is the weakest below ~24px. Nine cells with gaps turn to texture at
favicon size and the tinted cells disappear entirely. It is the strongest of the
three on a deck slide and the worst on a Discord server list. If you love it, it
needs a separate simplified small-size variant — that is a real cost, not a
detail.

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
|-------|-----|-----|
| Graphite | `#14161B` | Core. All marks, all body text. Never pure black. |
| Paper | `#F7F7F4` | Ground. Never pure white for large fields. |
| Signal | `#E04E39` | Accent. One use per view. Never a fill behind body text. |
| Rule | `#D8D8D1` | Hairlines, dividers, borders. |
| Quiet | `#8A8C87` | Captions, mono labels, secondary. |

Signal is deliberately warmer and more orange than Valorant's `#FF4655`. It
keeps competitive heat without reading as Riot merchandise or as a team
colourway. Paper plus graphite does the institutional work; Signal is the only
place the temperature rises.

**Alternate accent, if you want to go further against the grain:** a cool
analytic blue in place of Signal. It reads more consultancy and less competitor.
I recommend Signal, but the blue is a defensible choice and worth seeing before
you lock.

## Type

| Role | Face | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| Wordmark & headings | **Archivo** 600/700 | Tight tracking. Institutional authority — carries the "firm, not roster" half. |
| Technical layer | **JetBrains Mono** 400/500 | Uppercase, wide tracking. Match IDs, stat callouts, labels, captions. Carries the game-native half. |
| Body | Archivo 400 | |

The split is the strategy: the grotesk speaks to owners, the mono speaks to
players. Two families, no more.

## Usage

- **Clearspace:** one bracket arm-length (30 units at the mark's 100-unit grid)
  on all sides. Nothing enters it.
- **Minimum size:** 16px for the symbol. Below that, use the lettermark alone.
- **One ink:** every mark must survive as a single flat colour — `mark.svg` with
  all fills set to one value. Tested for embroidery, etching and fax-grade print.
- **Reversed:** `mark-knockout.svg` on grounds darker than Graphite 60%.
- **Co-brand:** `frame-cobrand.svg`. Partner mark fits inside the 42×42 centre
  area and never exceeds it.

### Don't

- Don't outline, bevel, gradient or add a glow.
- Don't rotate the frame. Its orthogonality is the whole point.
- Don't recolour the brackets to Signal. Signal is the centre only.
- Don't set the wordmark in anything but Archivo.
- Don't reintroduce "The" or the plural in the lockup.
